## Wave rules

1. Compare each family on settlement clarity, source permission by intended use, historical outcome/quote coverage, time-aligned forecast availability, quote granularity, geography, sample support, and measured or evidenced volume.
2. Exclude or block any cohort with ambiguous settlement, unresolved source use, missing forecast vintage, or unverified coverage; record the reason.
3. Select later waves only after the first selected family passes event-level chronological calibration; require specialized designs where horizons or joint outcomes differ.
4. Keep estimates and claims separate: provider documentation, vendor claims, synthetic tests, and authorized measured samples are different evidence classes.
5. Do not collect raw records until source use, retention, and a qualified second reviewer are recorded.
